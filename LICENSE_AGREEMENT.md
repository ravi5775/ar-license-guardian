license key) and transmits it to Vendor's activation service solely for

a. Enforcing the single-deployment restriction in §2;

No end-user personal data is included in the fingerprint. This mechanism
is a contractual evidence tool, not a technical DRM guarantee — Client
acknowledges that a determined party with source access can disable it,
and agrees that doing so constitutes wilful breach under §7.
